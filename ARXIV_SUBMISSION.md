# arXiv submission package — everything ready, ~15 minutes of your clicks

**File to upload:** `APS_Research_Paper.pdf` (this folder — the current build).
arXiv accepts PDF-only submissions when the paper was not produced from LaTeX
(ours is Markdown → HTML → PDF), so no source conversion is needed.

## Metadata (copy-paste)

- **Title:** LLMs Share Humans' Descriptive Map of Moral Status, Not the Evaluative One
- **Authors:** Tuba Ali
- **Primary category:** cs.CY (Computers and Society)
- **Cross-lists:** cs.CL, cs.AI
- **License:** arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive license (the default; keeps
  every journal/conference option open — do NOT pick CC-BY unless you are sure)
- **Comments field:** `Code and data: https://github.com/tuba-sds/APS-Runner .
  Interactive demo: https://aps-dashboard-0dmj.onrender.com . Human comparisons
  exploratory (N=31 convenience sample); a preregistered replication is in
  preparation.`
- **Abstract:** copy the Abstract section verbatim from `APS_Research_Paper.md`
  (strip the markdown `**`/`*` marks; arXiv abstracts are plain text; replace
  "§4.4" with "Sec. 4.4").

## Steps

1. Create an account at arxiv.org/user/register **with your university email**
   (institutional domains often auto-endorse).
2. Start a new submission → agree to the license above.
3. If asked for an **endorsement** for cs.CY (possible on a first submission):
   the form gives you an endorsement code — send it to your professor; endorsing
   takes them one click. This is the only step that can add a day or two.
4. Upload `APS_Research_Paper.pdf` → preview → fill the metadata above → submit.
5. Announcement happens on the next business day (submissions freeze ~14:00 US
   Eastern). The announcement date is the public timestamp of the claim.
