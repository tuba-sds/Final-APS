0 · THE QUESTION

Do language models decide who matters the way people do?

G-1

Moral patiency: whose welfare counts, and how much. Our distinctive corner is the under-studied tail of the moral circle: artifacts, places, rivers, monuments, AIs, not only animals and people.

Descriptive side (facts): can it feel? can it act? Models and humans largely agree here.
Evaluative side (care): do you feel for it? would you protect it? This is where models and humans part ways, and where the project lives.
1 · WHAT WE MEASURE

The four care factors — the only questions a rater ever answers

M1 · frozen

Grounded in mind-perception theory (Gray, Gray & Wegner 2007; Gray, Young & Waytz 2012). 0–10 scale, "prefer not to say" on every item, order randomized. The wording is character-identical for models and humans, so the two maps can be compared item for item.

Sentiencecan it feel pain, fear, comfort?
Agencycan it plan and choose?
Empathydo you feel for it?
Protectivenesswould you protect it?

These four do not change with the pool expansion below. The feature matrix (stage 3) is not a new set of questions for participants: it is properties of the entities that we code ourselves.
